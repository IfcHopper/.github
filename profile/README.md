<h1 align="center">IfcHopper</h1>

<p align="center"><b>openBIM in Rhino and Grasshopper</b><br>
Create, read, update and delete data directly in native IFC files.</p>

---

**IfcHopper** (formerly IfcHopperShell) is an open-source Grasshopper plugin for Rhino 8. It works on IFC files as they are, without converting them to another format: build IFC models from Grasshopper, explore existing files, edit them in place and write them back, keeping everything you don't touch unchanged.

- **Read** IFC2X3, IFC4 and IFC4X3 files: spatial tree, elements, geometry, types, materials, property sets, classifications, openings.
- **Create** buildings and IFC 4.3 infrastructure (bridges, roads, railways, marine facilities) with any IFC element class.
- **Edit in place**, matched by GlobalId.
- **Write** IFC4X3_ADD2, IFC4 or IFC2X3.

> **Status: 1.0 beta.** Try it on your models and tell us what works and what doesn't.
